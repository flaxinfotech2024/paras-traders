About Us

Paras Traders was established in 1981 and has since become a leading manufacturer of high-quality diamond polishing tools. Our product range includes Diamond Scief Saran and Diamond Ceramics Bruter Wheels, designed to meet the highest industry standards. Additionally, we are authorized stockists and dealers of various types of industrial synthetic diamond powders, including Yellow, Green, and Black Diamond Powders.

We are proud to be authorized dealers for CUTALL SUPERABRASIVE, a renowned company from China, and also a major stockist of IND Yellow Diamond Powder in all grades and mesh sizes.

With decades of experience in the synthetic industrial diamond powder industry, we specialize in providing HI-TECH Diamond Powder, which is widely used in diamond polishing tools, ceramic abrasive tools, and industrial diamond tools. This versatile powder is ideal for cutting, polishing, and grinding applications.

Our company is committed to delivering the best quality products, offering excellent customer service, and maintaining honesty and integrity in all business dealings. At Paras Trads, hard work and a dedication to excellence are at the core of our mission.
